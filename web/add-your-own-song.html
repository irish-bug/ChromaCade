Add Your Own Song Coming soon

Tutor and Simon modes can already play any song you'd like your child to learn -- this page will make adding one as simple as playing it once.

The planned flow:

  1. Play the melody on ChromaCade's own note buttons, one time through.
  2. Listen back to what it recorded, right on the device -- no separate app needed.
  3. If a note gets fumbled, there'll be a way to undo just the last one, not restart the whole recording.
  4. Happy with it? Give it a name here and it's saved, ready to pick from the Tutor and Simon menus.

Songs added this way stay private to your device -- they're not published anywhere, which matters if it's someone else's copyrighted song: playing it on your own instrument is fine, this just keeps that separate from anything shared publicly.
