Add Your Own Song

Build a song here, then save it -- it shows up in both Tutor mode's song list and as a Simon-mode sequence source, exactly like any of the built-in songs. Add notes by clicking the letter buttons below, typing their letters on your keyboard, or dragging a letter onto the staff at the spot you want it. Click any note already on the staff to change its octave, sharp/flat, or duration, or to delete it.
